calibrated uncertainty plus an explicit applicability-domain check, so the
model degrades gracefully outside its training domain instead of failing
silently. Includes a from-scratch graph neural net comparison, a genuine
3,896-compound prospective validation, and extensions to two more cardiac
channels plus two non-cardiotoxicity endpoints.
