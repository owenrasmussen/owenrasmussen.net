Lazy Booking is an AI travel agent: tell it the trip you want in plain language and
it plans the flights, the hotel, and the itinerary, then books everything in a
single checkout. Built on Next.js, OpenAI, Duffel (flights + hotels), Clerk
(auth), Supabase (persistence), and Stripe (payments). Currently in beta,
running against the Duffel sandbox.
